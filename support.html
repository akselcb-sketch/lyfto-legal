Get help with Lyfto.

Last updated: 2026-06-04

Lyfto is a workout-tracking app for iOS with an AI coach that has read your whole training history. If something isn't working or you have a question, this page is the fastest way to get help.

Contact us

Email akselcornelius@icloud.com and we'll get back to you, usually within 1–2 business days. Lyfto is made by Aksel Cornelius Bjorland.

To help us reach a fix faster, please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what you expected versus what happened. Screenshots are welcome.

Bringing your history from another app

Open Profile → Import Data. Lyfto reads CSV exports from Strong, Hevy, and Fitbod — your workouts, weights, and notes come across with you.

Your data isn't syncing across devices

Make sure you're signed in to the same account on each device and have a network connection. Lyfto saves everything on-device first and syncs in the background, so nothing is lost — your data catches up once you're back online.

Exporting or deleting your data

Profile → Export Data gives you a standard JSON file with everything. Profile → Delete Account permanently removes your data from our servers. See the Privacy Policy for the full details.

The AI coach isn't responding

The coach needs a network connection. If it still isn't answering, fully close and reopen the app. If the problem continues, email us with the rough time it happened so we can check our logs.

Heart rate or Apple Health isn't showing

Grant Lyfto permission in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Lyfto. Heart rate is read live during a workout on supported devices.
